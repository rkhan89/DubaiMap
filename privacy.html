Privacy policy

Koko is a shared food map for you and the people you choose. This explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to get it back or have it deleted. The short version: we keep what you put in so the app can work, you decide who sees each place, and we don't sell your data, show ads or use trackers.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Your profile: display name, handle, short tagline and avatar.

What you add

  • Places you save or log: name, area, pin on the map, kind of place, ratings, notes, dates, meal tags, photos and captions.
  • Who each place is for (just you, or the crews you pick) and the people you tag as being with you.
  • Your crews, crew names and mottos, plans you make and your RSVPs, photo bookmarks, scrapbook covers, and the stickers, layouts and notes added to scrapbook pages.
  • Links and text you paste into "Add from link or text", and the place it finds. The link itself is kept private to you.
  • Shares waiting in your Inbox (ones you save for later, share while offline, or share before signing in): the link and text, visible only to you, until you add or remove them.

Technical information

  • A sign-in session stored on your phone so you stay signed in. Your settings and a copy of your scrapbook are also kept on your phone so the app works offline.
  • When the app looks up a link, our server logs the kind of link, whether it worked and how long it took. It does not log the link or text itself.
  • Our hosting providers keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period for security and reliability.

Feedback and error reports

  • Feedback you send from Settings, with the app version and your phone's model, browser, language and screen size.
  • When the app hits an error on your phone, a report of what broke (the error and where in the app's code) with the same phone details. Never anything you typed or saved. Only while you're signed in.

What we don't collect

  • Location history. Check-ins use your phone's location on your phone only, to confirm you're at the place; the location isn't sent to us or stored. The one exception: when a check-in catches a critter, the catch is saved with the spot's name and roughly where you were (to about 100 m), and your crews can see it. "Show me on the map" also stays on your phone.
  • Photo location data. Photos are resized on your phone before upload, which removes hidden location and camera details.
  • Your contacts, browsing, advertising IDs or anything from other apps. There are no ads, analytics trackers or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To run Koko for you: show your map and scrapbook, share what you choose with your crews, deliver notifications in the app, and look up places from links. This is needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • To keep it safe and working: limit abuse (for example, how many links one person can look up per hour), fix problems and protect accounts. This is in our legitimate interest and yours.
  • With your permission: reminders on your phone, and your location for check-ins. You can switch these off at any time in your phone or in Settings.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Just me places and photos are visible to you only.
  • A place you share with a crew is visible to the people in that crew. A crewmate only sees it through a crew you're both in, and if you leave a crew, what you shared only with them becomes yours alone again.
  • People you tag on a visit can see that visit and its photos, in a Tagged scrapbook of their own. Tagging someone also shares the visit with a crew you're both in, and the app tells you which before you save.
  • If you're tagged on a visit you can add your own rating and note. They're seen by the people on that visit and by the crews it was shared with, while you're still in them; leave a crew and your ratings stop showing to it.
  • Every visit is a page in your scrapbook, and in the book of each crew it's shared with. Notes and stickers added to a page are seen by whoever can see that page.
  • Your crews see your name, handle and avatar. People outside your crews don't.
  • Links you paste in are never shown to your crew; at most they see "from TikTok" or "from Google Maps".
  • These rules are enforced on our database, not just in the app.

Services that process data for us

We use a small number of providers to run Koko. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes above.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and private photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosting the app and the server that looks up links.
  • Google: when you add from a link or name, the link or search text is sent to Google Places to find the place. Google also serves the app's fonts, and handles sign-in if you choose Google.
  • jsDelivr: delivers part of the app's code to your browser.
  • Email delivery: sends your sign-in codes.
  • TikTok: when you share a TikTok link, we ask TikTok for that post's public caption. TikTok sees the request comes from our server, not from you.
  • Anthropic: when you share a TikTok or paste a caption, the caption's text is sent to Anthropic's Claude to work out which place it mentions. Nothing else about you is sent, and under Anthropic's commercial terms that text isn't used to train its models.
  • Stripe and PayPal: only if you choose to support Koko. They handle the payment; we never see your card or account details.

Some of these providers process data outside the United Arab Emirates. Where they do, they protect it under their own data processing terms.

How long we keep it

  • Your places, photos and profile are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Deleting a visit or photo removes it straight away. Deleting your account removes your data from Koko straight away; copies in our providers' backups expire within 30 days.
  • Link lookups are cached for up to 30 days, and only for you: where a Google Maps link leads, or the names of the places a TikTok mentions. Captions themselves aren't stored. Server logs are kept for a few days.
  • Error reports are deleted after 90 days, and feedback after a year.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself:

  • See and export: Settings → Export backup gives you your logs and photos as one file.
  • Correct: edit any visit, your profile or your handle.
  • Delete: delete any visit or photo, or Settings → Delete my account to remove everything.

For anything else, contact . If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live (in the UAE, the UAE Data Office).

Children

Koko is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. Access is checked by rules on the database itself, photos are stored privately and opened with links that expire after a short time, and sign-in uses one-time codes rather than passwords. No system is perfectly secure, so if you think something's wrong, tell us.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
