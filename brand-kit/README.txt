KOKO BRAND KIT

Colours
  Gold   #E8A92B
  Brown  #2B1A10
  Cream  #FFF4E8
  Dark   #1C1410 (dark theme background used in the logo tests)

brand/      Source SVGs. All letters are outlined shapes, no fonts needed.
  koko-wordmark-brown.svg   wordmark for light backgrounds
  koko-wordmark-cream.svg   wordmark for dark backgrounds
  koko-pin.svg              the ring and dot on its own (loaders, small marks)
  koko-icon.svg             app icon, ko monogram on gold
  koko-icon-dark.svg        app icon, ko monogram on dark
icons/      Web and PWA icons
  icon-192.png, icon-512.png                 purpose any
  icon-maskable-192.png, icon-maskable-512.png   purpose maskable
  icon-monochrome-512.png                    purpose monochrome (themed icons)
  apple-touch-icon-180.png                   iOS home screen
  favicon.svg, favicon.ico, favicon-32.png, favicon-16.png   the pin, simplified for tiny sizes
social/     Link preview image, 1200 x 630
android/    Adaptive icon layers for a future native build (foreground, monochrome, background colour)
store/      Play Store 512 and App Store 1024 icons (no transparency)

Do not recolour, stretch or redraw. If another size is needed, export it from the SVG, not from a PNG.
