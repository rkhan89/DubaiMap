1. Device checks (open this page on your iPhone and Android phone)

Google Sans Flex has a roundness axis (ROND). Each row is the same word at roundness 0 and 100. If both look the same on a phone, the axis isn't working there and the rounded headings would fall back to square ends.

KokoROND 100
KarakROND 0, 800
KarakROND 100, 800

The Android app will use the phone's System WebView, which is Chrome's engine. Checking it in Chrome on Android is the closest test until the Capacitor build exists.

2. Colour

Gold only for Check in and ratings. Ink for selected states, selected tabs and the selection ring. Sea-teal means shared with a crew and always comes with the crew icon, on paper or inside an ink outline. "Just me" is ink with a lock.

12. Icon set options

The same 23 icons the app uses most, in three sets. The app stays on the first until you pick.

  • Material Symbols Outlined: today's set, now self-hosted at 147 KB. Familiar and complete, but generic (Google's own look).
  • Phosphor (MIT): six weights, friendly rounded geometry, 9,000+ icons including food. Closest to the rounded wordmark.
  • Lucide (ISC): crisp 2 px strokes, very consistent, about 1,500 icons. Neutral and modern; fewer food icons.
